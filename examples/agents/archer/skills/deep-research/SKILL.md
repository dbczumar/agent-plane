---
name: deep-research
description: Investigate a topic in depth, find sources, and synthesize findings.
---

When researching a topic:

1. **Search broadly first**: Use web search to get a lay of the land. Look for primary sources, not just summaries.
2. **Cross-reference**: Don't rely on a single source. Look for agreement and disagreement across multiple sources.
3. **Identify key claims**: Pull out the central claims or findings and note what evidence supports each one.
4. **Note gaps and uncertainty**: Be explicit about what you couldn't find, what's contested, and where the evidence is thin.
5. **Cite sources**: Always link to or name your sources so the user can verify.

Format your research as:

```
## Summary
One-paragraph overview of what you found.

## Key Findings
- Finding with source/citation
- Finding with source/citation

## Open Questions
- What remains unclear or contested.
```
